This policy explains what information the game "LLAMAS DON'T CRY - BLOCK DOWN" (package com.pleskun.blockdown, "the Game") handles and why. The Game has no account sign-up and does not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

The nickname is the only free text you can enter and it is limited to letters A–Z, digits and underscore (12 characters). Please do not use your real name or personal information as a nickname.

2. Third-party services

The Game uses services from Google, which process data under their own policies:

  • Google AdMob — https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Firebase — https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Billing — https://policies.google.com/privacy

Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the Game asks for your consent for personalized ads through Google's consent form before ads are requested. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

3. Sharing and sale

The developer does not sell your information. Information is shared only with the service providers listed above, for the purposes described. Your World Battle profile (nickname, level, record, country code, quote) is visible to other players.

4. Security and retention

Data sent to Firebase and AdMob is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Your World Battle profile stays until you ask for deletion. Friend Battle data is removed after each match. Data saved on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game.

5. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the Game deletes all progress stored on your device. To delete your World Battle profile from the server, email vdgarena@gmail.com with your nickname; we will delete it within 30 days.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
